FIELDS OF THE IRRAWADDY — CLASSIC FARMING-SIM AUDIO REDESIGN

AUDIO DIRECTION
The previous synthetic oscillator-style tracks have been replaced with original, sampled-instrument compositions built around the broad musical language of classic Harvest Moon-era farming games: memorable short melodic phrases, gentle chord progressions, steady arpeggiated accompaniment, soft bass movement, long looping arrangements, and distinct moods for different parts of the day.

This update does not contain Harvest Moon recordings or copy any Harvest Moon melody. All arrangements and sound effects are newly composed/generated for Fields of the Irrawaddy.

BACKGROUND MUSIC
- public/audio/music/dawn.ogg — warm, bright flute melody over nylon guitar arpeggios and soft piano support.
- public/audio/music/day.ogg — livelier flute theme, steel-string guitar, rounded bass and restrained marimba accents.
- public/audio/music/dusk.ogg — slower violin-led melody, nylon guitar and mellow sustained piano harmony.
- public/audio/music/night.ogg — sparse music-box melody, gentle guitar and soft string backing in a minor-key mood.

The tracks are approximately 73–98 seconds long. They use sampled General MIDI instrument timbres, stereo placement, light reverb/chorus and a crossfaded loop seam. Existing in-game time-of-day selection and crossfading remain unchanged.

SOUND EFFECTS
The existing effect filenames and event names are preserved, so the current gameplay calls continue to work:
- hoe, plant, water, harvest, dead
- request, buy, sell, talk, error
- cast, bite, catch, miss
- cook, eat, sleep, footstep

Effects now emphasize a physical source: low soil impacts and loose grit for hoeing, granules for planting, multi-layer splashes for watering, leaf rustle for harvesting, quiet dirt impacts for footsteps, and bubbling/sizzle textures for cooking. Buy/sell/catch/sleep cues use soft sampled instrument accents rather than sharp electronic beeps. Each effect has a short fade to avoid clicks and leaves headroom for the in-game SFX volume bus.

AUDIO SETTINGS
The existing independent background-music and sound-effect sliders are unchanged. Existing localStorage keys and settings are preserved. Music continues through the music bus; gameplay sounds continue through the effects bus.

All audio files are delivered as OGG assets under public/audio. The source project does not need to download or load the SoundFont at runtime; it is used only to render the delivered audio files.
