# Paid asset pack integration

These eleven PNG sprite sheets were supplied by the project owner in `Paid Pack.zip`. They are kept intact in this folder. `asset-manifest.json` records the frame grids used by the Phaser loader.

The game uses all eleven sheets: a travelling character, animated sheep and shadow, fence tiles, animated fire, a small animated house and door, an animated outhouse door, paired 48px tree tiles, a water/pond animation, plant accents and terrain tiles. The complete sheets remain available for additional tile-map editing and future seasonal/environment work.

The project does not claim ownership of the supplied artwork. Keep the original purchase/license information with your project and check the pack's terms before distributing the game or raw asset files publicly.
