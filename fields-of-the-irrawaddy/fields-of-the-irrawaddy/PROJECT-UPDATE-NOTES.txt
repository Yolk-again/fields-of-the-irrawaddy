FIELDS OF THE IRRAWADDY — MAP & NPC FARMING UPDATE

MAP
- Added two 5 x 3 NPC rice plots for Ko Aung and Ma Thiri.
- Both plots use the same ground tiles, crop stages, watering states, and fence/gate geometry as the player's plot.
- Reworked the shared farm access lane, market and monastery forecourts, granary/harvest yard, paddy edge, and lotus-pond garden.
- Redrew the toddy palm and banana plant sprite assets in tools/pixel_art and regenerated the terrain/art manifest.

NPC FARMING LOOP
- Morning route works tile-by-tile: hoe, plant seed, water.
- Crops grow on the shared 24-hour in-game clock and need four successful daily watering cycles to mature.
- On the mature day, the NPC harvests each crop tile and carries the rice to the market.
- NPC harvest batches are sold independently of the player's inventory, then the NPC plot resets for the next cycle.
- NPC crop state and mid-harvest sale progress are stored in the save data.

EXPANSION DESIGN NOTE
- The starting NPC plots intentionally match the player's 5 x 3 plot. A paid expansion is recommended as a later progression upgrade rather than enlarging the starting map immediately. No expansion price or upgrade UI has been added in this update.

RUN
- npm install
- npm run build
- npm run dev

LATEST UPDATE — ROADSIDE LAMPS & DAY/NIGHT LIGHTING

LATEST UPDATE — GLOBAL SOUND MUTE FIX

- The Music icon is now a true global sound toggle: it immediately sets the shared Web Audio output bus to zero and suspends the AudioContext, stopping currently-playing notes, birds, crickets, water/wind ambience, and in-progress one-shot effects.
- New bird/cricket/music scheduling and one-shot sound effects are guarded while muted.
- Phaser's own Sound Manager is muted/unmuted by the same control, covering any native Phaser sounds added now or later.

LATEST UPDATE — FISHING & HOME COOKING

- Added a riverbank fishing activity with three accessible bank spots, purchased fishing rod, cast/wait/reel timing, and a short bite window. Fish species vary with the in-game time: Silver Barb, River Catfish, and Golden Carp.
- Added fishing rod and fish inventory tracking, including separate counts for all three fish types. Fishing equipment, fish counts, and cooked meals are included in save data; older saves load with empty fishing/cooking progress.
- Added an outdoor home cooking hearth on the grass beside the player’s home yard (kept clear of the main road and farm gate) and three recipes: Fish & Rice Bowl, River Catfish Mohinga, and Golden Carp Curry. Recipes consume harvested rice and the appropriate fish.
- Cooked meals can be sold at the village market for more than the raw rice value. Raw fish can also be sold by species (Silver Barb 4 coins, Catfish 6, Golden Carp 8), so players can choose an immediate sale or save a catch for cooking. Players can eat a meal to receive a temporary fishing-luck bonus until the next in-game day.
- Added clickable recipe cards and keyboard controls (1–3 to cook, Q to eat, E to close), plus market actions (1 buy seeds, 2 sell rice, 3 buy rod, 4 sell meals).
- Expanded the bilingual Help guide with fishing and cooking cards, and expanded the backpack resource display to show fish variety, meals, and rod ownership.
- Added player cast/reel and cooking/eating task animations, plus original procedural cast, bite, catch, miss, cooking, and eating effects. The existing global mute control remains in charge of music, birds, ambience, and all gameplay sounds.
- Fishing spots were checked against map collisions; the middle bank point was moved away from the granary footprint. Player movement is locked during an active cast so the fishing line and bobber remain attached to the selected spot.

HOUSE INTERIOR UPDATE
---------------------
The player's house is now enterable. Walk to the front door and press E.
Inside, use WASD / arrow keys to move and E near the bed, storage chest, or
cooking hearth. Press ESC or use the red close control to leave the house.

Sleep schedule:
- Sleep between 06:00 and 11:59 -> wake at 18:00 the same in-game day.
- Sleep between 12:00 and 18:59 -> wake at 21:00 the same in-game day.
- Sleep from 19:00 through the pre-dawn hours -> wake at 07:00 the next day.

The storage chest transfers rice seeds, harvested rice, all fish species, and
cooked meals between the backpack and persistent home storage. The cooker is
now indoors beside the kitchen hearth; recipes and meal buffs are unchanged.
Sleeping advances the shared village clock and synchronizes crop growth, daily
requests, NPC schedules, lighting, and ambient audio.
