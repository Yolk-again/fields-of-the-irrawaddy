FIELDS OF THE IRRAWADDY — ORIGINAL PIXEL ASSET PACK (V5)

The core Burmese village artwork is original project artwork, generated by Python/Pillow
scripts in `tools/pixel_art/`. The separately supplied paid asset sheets are kept intact in
`paid_pack/` and documented there. Art is authored at compact pixel sizes and rendered in
Phaser at 2x. To rebuild the project-generated assets:

    python3 -m pip install pillow
    python3 tools/pixel_art/build.py

Character sheets: `characters/*_sheet.png` are 4 columns x 14 rows of 16x26 frames.
Rows: down walk, up walk, side walk, general work/play, sit/rest, plant, water, harvest,
fish, cook, gesture/offer, bow/homage, eat, alms-bowl carry. Every named villager retains the Burmese
clothing palette (longyi/htamein, thanaka, khamauk, gaung baung, monk robe).

Animals: chicken = 6 frames (idle, peck, recover, two walk steps, wing flutter);
duck = 4 frames (idle, head dip, two waddle steps); water buffalo = 4 frames with
hoof alternation/head bob/tail swish; common myna = 4 wing frames.

Agriculture: crop stages 0-3 plus 4-frame wind-sway sheets for stages 1-3.
Village ambience: myna birds fly around the canopy during the day; fireflies pulse in
shrub/grass clearings at night; palms, banana plants and fruit trees sway gently.

Burmese visual identity: teak and bamboo stilt houses, lacquer chest, woven floor mat,
longyi-style wall textile, terracotta water jar, brass/clay oil lamp, khamauk, thanaka,
gaung baung, monastery, golden zedi, toddy palms, banana plants, lotus water, rice fields.

The separate `ASSET-PACK-REVIEW.md` documents the three earlier reference packs whose
redistribution terms did not permit copying their images. The owner-supplied paid sheets
are the exception: they are included intact in `paid_pack/` and integrated into the game.
See `paid_pack/README.md` for sheet grids, current usages and licence reminders.
