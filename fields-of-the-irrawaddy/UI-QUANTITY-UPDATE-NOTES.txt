FIELDS OF THE IRRAWADDY — INTERACTION AND QUANTITY UPDATE

House interior
- Removed the permanently visible bed, storage chest, and cooking hearth labels.
- Removed the always-on welcome/room description.
- Added contextual prompts above the player when close to the bed, storage chest, cooking hearth, or exit doorway.
- Prompts are localized in English and Burmese.
- Moved the outdoor E-to-enter prompt upward slightly.

Storage chest
- Added per-item quantity selection using minus and plus buttons.
- Hold Shift while clicking minus/plus to change the selected amount by 10.
- STORE and TAKE now transfer only the selected amount, capped at the amount available on the relevant side.
- Existing stored contents and save-data structure are unchanged.

Village market
- Selling rice, cooked meals, and fish now opens a quantity selector.
- The selector shows the available quantity and provides minus, plus, MAX, SELL AMOUNT, and CANCEL controls.
- Only the selected quantity is sold.
- Existing item prices are preserved: rice 5 coins; Silver Barb 4; Catfish 6; Golden Carp 8; Fish & Rice Bowl 20; Mohinga 24; Golden Carp Curry 20.
- For mixed fish or meal sales, common/first-listed varieties are consumed first, matching the previous all-sell order.
