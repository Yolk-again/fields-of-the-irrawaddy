FIELDS OF THE IRRAWADDY — HOME INTERIOR GUIDE

ENTERING YOUR HOME
Walk to the front of the player's wooden house and press E when the
ENTER YOUR HOME prompt appears.

MOVING INSIDE
Use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk around the room. Move near a piece
of furniture and press E, or click it when close enough.

BED — SLEEP
- Sleep from 06:00–11:59: wake at 18:00 the same day.
- Sleep from 12:00–18:59: wake at 21:00 the same day.
- Sleep from 19:00–05:59: wake at 07:00 the next in-game day.
Sleeping advances the same village clock used by crops, NPC routines,
daily requests, lighting, and ambient audio.

STORAGE CHEST
Use STORE to move all of a listed item from your backpack into the chest.
Use TAKE to move the stored stack back to your backpack. The chest saves
its contents automatically with the rest of your game. It stores rice seeds,
harvested rice, Silver Barb, River Catfish, Golden Carp, and cooked meals.

COOKING HEARTH
The cooking hearth is inside the house. Press E beside it to open recipes.
Existing fish-and-rice recipes, meal storage, and meal buffs are preserved.

EXIT
Use ESC or the red close control to return to the village outside.
