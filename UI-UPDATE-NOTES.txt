FIELDS OF THE IRRAWADDY — UI POLISH UPDATE

Changes in this update

1. Removed the permanent bottom-left keyboard hint (WASD MOVE / E TALK).
   Movement and interaction guidance remains available in the Help panel.

2. Removed the CROP DAY chip shown to the right of the farming hotbar.
   The internal day counter and all crop-growth/day progression logic remain intact.

3. Fixed the Village Request Board modal heading layout.
   The title/subtitle are moved down slightly, use a narrower heading size, and
   have constrained wrapping so they no longer crowd the upper edge of the tab.

4. Added separate audio controls.
   Click the music-note button in the top-right HUD to open Audio Settings.
   Background Music and Sound Effects have independent 0–100% sliders.
   Both values are saved in browser localStorage and restored on the next visit.
   Ambient music/water/breeze/birds/crickets follow Background Music; action,
   interaction, farming, fishing, cooking and reward sounds follow Sound Effects.

5. Updated the Market and Village Request prompts.
   Both now use the same dark, cream-text, outlined prompt style as the house
   entrance prompt. They are centered on screen and positioned at y=286,
   slightly higher than the house prompt to leave more space above the hotbar.

## House instruction and audio slider follow-up

- Removed the in-room WASD / E / ESC instruction banner from the house interior.
- Removed the house-specific Escape key handler. ESC no longer exits the house; use the red close button or the doorway.
- Updated English and Burmese house help text so it no longer tells players to press ESC to leave.
- Reworked audio slider input to use scene-level pointer hit testing while the Audio Settings modal is open. Clicking or dragging either track now updates its percentage, saves that setting independently, and applies the corresponding Web Audio gain bus.
