# Crop Growth Schedule

The player's farm and the village NPC farms use three watering cycles before a crop is ready.

- **Day 1:** Prepare the soil, plant the seed, and water it.
- **Day 2:** Water for the second time.
- **Day 3:** Water for the third time.
- **Day 4:** The crop reaches its mature stage and can be harvested.

For the player's crops, each growth stage advances after 24 in-game hours have elapsed since that plot's previous watering. Watering becomes available when that cycle completes. NPC farm plots advance on the shared in-game day transition.
