# Fields of the Irrawaddy — Story Direction

## Opening story added in this update

The new-world opening cinematic introduces three beats:

1. **Home again:** the player returns to their family's village beside the Irrawaddy.
2. **The quiet canal:** Ko Aung explains that the old irrigation canal is low and the village fields need help.
3. **A village together:** Ma Thiri keeps the market moving while neighbours share what they can; the player is invited to grow a harvest, answer requests, and help prepare for a village harvest celebration.

The cinematic appears after first-time character creation. Existing save files skip it so returning players can continue immediately. It can be advanced or skipped.

## Recommended longer story progression

- **Chapter 1 — Settling In:** meet Ko Aung, Ma Thiri, the child, and the monk; repair the player's home and grow the first rice harvest.
- **Chapter 2 — Water for the Fields:** complete small deliveries, clear irrigation sections, and unlock more plots as the village canal improves.
- **Chapter 3 — Neighbours' Stories:** give recurring characters personal requests tied to existing systems: fish for a family meal, ingredients for Ma Thiri's stall, seeds for a neighbour, and materials for home repairs.
- **Chapter 4 — The Village Market:** restore the market's variety by selling produce and completing weekly request-board goals. New stalls and decorations appear as progress is made.
- **Chapter 5 — The Harvest Celebration:** a seasonal event that uses crops, cooked meals, decorations, and friendship progress. The village changes visually as preparations advance.

## Keep the story connected to play

Each story beat should unlock a concrete objective, use an existing system, and visibly change the village or a character's routine. Prefer short conversations and illustrated moments over long text walls. Let the player decide when to farm, fish, cook, trade, or explore between story objectives.
