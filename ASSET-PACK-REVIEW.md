# Third-party asset pack review

The following three packs were provided for review while refreshing the art and animation
systems in **Fields of the Irrawaddy**. Their contents were inspected as visual references,
but no third-party image files were copied into the game or redistributed in this project.
All new shipped artwork was produced by the project's own generators in `tools/pixel_art/`.

## SuperRetroRanch Free Tier v3.5

Useful reference ideas: crop growth variety, multiple tool-use poses, and the breadth of
small farming interactions. The supplied licence explicitly restricts the Free Tier to
non-commercial projects and says the assets may not be distributed or sold directly,
even when modified. Because the user needs a complete distributable project ZIP, its PNGs
were not copied into this project. The licence requests credit to “Gif” if possible; no
credit is claimed because none of its assets are shipped here.

## Farm RPG FREE 16x16 — Tiny Asset Pack

Useful reference ideas: compact 16x16 character proportions, walk/idle sheet organization,
small animal frames, fences, and homely furniture. The supplied RAR contained character,
animal, house/interior, chest, fence, crop, road, and tree images, but no license, README,
or terms file was present in the archive. Permission to redistribute those files could
not be verified, so none were copied into the project.

## Cute Fantasy Free

Useful reference ideas: animation readability and clear silhouettes. Its included readme
allows use and modification in non-commercial projects but prohibits redistribution or
resale, including modified assets. Its fantasy/chibi outlines also differ noticeably from
the existing Burmese village pixel-art style. No images from this pack were copied into
this project.

## What was actually added

The refreshed assets are original drawings generated by this project's own Python/Pillow
scripts. They retain the existing Burmese visual identity: longyi and htamein, thanaka,
khamauk, gaung baung, monk robes, teak and bamboo stilt houses, lacquer details, woven
textiles, terracotta jars, Burmese-style household lamps, rice fields, palms, banana plants,
and the Irrawaddy village environment. See `public/assets/README.md` and
`tools/pixel_art/` for the generation source.


## Newly supplied paid pack — integrated

The project owner subsequently supplied `Paid Pack.zip` and explicitly requested that its assets, tile sheets and animation sheets be used. The eleven original transparent PNG sheets are now included under `public/assets/paid_pack/`. They are loaded using their native grids: 16×16 character/animal/effect/terrain tiles, 48×48 tree and water tiles, 96×112 house frames, and 36×48 toilet frames.

The current game now uses the pack's walking character, animated sheep and matching shadow, fence tiles for a sheep paddock, animated campfire, chimney-smoke house, outhouse, fruit trees, pond/water frames, plant tiles and terrain details. The existing Burmese NPC designs, stilt houses, rice farming, fishing/cooking, and village systems remain in place.

No licence or purchase-terms document was included inside `Paid Pack.zip`, so the project preserves the files supplied by the owner but does not make any claim about redistribution rights. Keep the purchase receipt/licence with the project and check the pack's terms before publishing the raw assets or distributing a public build.
